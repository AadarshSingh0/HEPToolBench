# AI-Assisted Development Disclosure

This repository was developed with assistance from large language models.
OpenAI's ChatGPT and Anthropic's Claude were used as development aids while
drafting and refining some benchmark tasks, response schemas, expected
artifacts, scoring logic, software, analysis utilities, and documentation.
They also assisted with debugging and with reviewing proposed changes.

The language models were not used as judges of benchmark responses. Reported
scores and strict pass/fail outcomes are produced by the released,
task-specific deterministic scorers.

The project author defined the scientific objectives and benchmark scope,
selected the evaluated tasks and model deployments, reviewed the final task
definitions and scoring criteria, tested the incorporated code, ran the
benchmark, inspected the resulting artifacts, and made the final research and
software decisions. AI-generated suggestions and code changes were subject to
author review and testing. The author remains responsible for the repository
and for the scientific claims based on its results.

The released tasks, schemas, scorers, model responses, results, and analysis
scripts are provided so that the benchmark design and reported measurements
can be independently inspected and reproduced.
